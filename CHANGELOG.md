## [0.1.0] - 2026-02-11

### Added
- 12 compression styles: Clean, Classic, Opto, Vocal, Mastering, Bus, Punch, Pumping, Versatile, Smooth, Op-El, Vari-Mu
- Character saturation modes: Off, Tube, Diode, Bright with Drive (0-24 dB)
- 3-band sidechain EQ (low cut, bell, high cut)
- External sidechain input with audition
- 5 stereo modes: Normal, Mid, Side, M>S, S>M
- Stereo link (0-100%)
- Soft-knee compression with adjustable knee width (0-72 dB) and range limiting
- Lookahead up to 20 ms with automatic latency compensation
- Oversampling up to 32x (1x, 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x, 32x)
- Auto threshold with slow RMS follower
- Auto gain makeup compensation
- Dry/wet mix (0-200%) for parallel compression
- Undo/redo with full parameter history
- A/B comparison with instant parameter snapshot switching
- Delta listen mode
- MIDI trigger for external compression triggering
- Real-time spectrum analyzer with pre/post display
- Animated knee display with trail history
- Customizable meter scale (9/18/36/60/90 dB)
- 19 factory presets across Vocal, Drums, Bus, Mastering, and Creative categories
- Input/output gain and pan controls
- 37 automatable parameters
- VST3, AU, and Standalone formats (CLAP and AAX optional)
